# Release notes

Updates arrive by themselves through the Chrome Web Store. Your version is shown on `chrome://extensions`, next to the extension's name.

## 2.1.3 · 21 November 2026

Version 2. The Pinterest downloader became Mass Media Downloader, because the same extension now covers more sites.

- **New sites**: Reddit, X, Bluesky, Tumblr, Threads, Facebook and VSCO
- **Clip & GIF**: save a short section of a web video as a clip or a GIF, or one frame as a picture
- **My Library**: browse what you saved in one gallery, with collections, search, filters, favourites and tags
- **One plan, All sites**, covers every site in the extension. A key bought earlier for Pinterest keeps working for Pinterest.
- Pinterest stays as it was, with your options, saved-file records and licence

When an update asks for new permissions, Chrome switches the extension off until you accept them. Switch it back on in Chrome's extension page, then reload your tabs. Keep your existing installation to keep its data.
